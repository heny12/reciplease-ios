Reciplease turns the food you already have into dinner.

Scan your fridge or pantry, tell Reciplease what sounds good, and get a short list of Fresh Picks — distinct ideas with a time, a hook, and an honest pantry match. Open the one you want, cook it step by step, and save it to a cookbook only if you would make it again.

Plan the week, build a grocery list from the gaps, and keep dietary needs separate from tonight's craving. Free accounts include a weekly recipe allowance and unlimited fridge scans. Reciplease Pro adds recipe modifications, nutrition estimates, and a higher generation cap.

Your recipes, pantry, and meal plan stay on your phone.
